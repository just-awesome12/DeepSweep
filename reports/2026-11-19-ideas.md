# Deepsweep: ideas to stand out (19 November 2026)

**Verdict:** build the Daily Dig share card first. It is the smallest of the three, it uses what the game already has (a seeded daily run), and it is the only one that gives players something to post.

## Assumptions

No creator profile exists, so these are read from the repo, not stated by you:

- Solo developer working with an AI agent; budget close to zero.
- Plain JavaScript, one self-contained HTML file, no server.
- Released free on itch.io as a mobile-friendly web game; app-store and Steam wrappers are "not built yet".
- The game has no analytics, so no baseline numbers exist.

If any of these is wrong, the scores for Simplicity and Creator fit change.

## Market context (live pass, not a full trend scan)

- **Minesweeper roguelikes are no longer a gap.** Infinity Sweeper released on Steam on 7 May 2026 with polarized reviews; Tiled Gambit, Sweep Happens and a 21-floor, 100-relic title are listed for 2026; Dungeon Sweeper mixes the mechanic with an auto-battler (Steam and GameGeeker listings, read November 2026). "Minesweeper plus a run" is not a twist on its own any more.
- **Roguelike deckbuilders are crowded.** GameGeeker calls it one of the most crowded corners of Steam (2026). Adding more relics competes where the field is deepest.
- **Daily shared-result puzzles are holding, not growing.** Wordle averaged about 4.05 million daily users in 2025 and peaked that December (Udonis, read November 2026). I found no evidence of category growth in 2026.
- **Web portals:** revenue-share figures for Poki and CrazyGames conflict between sources. **Not verified**, so no number is used below.

What Deepsweep has that the search did not show in the others: tiles that are a trap or a cache with the clue not saying which, a streak multiplier on top, counted dead ends with a no-guess first floor, and a daily seed.

### Idea: Daily Dig share card

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Core loop** | Play the day's seeded run once → the end screen builds a spoiler-free picture of your run (one row per floor: safe flips, traps hit, caches found, best multiplier) → copy or share it → come back tomorrow to keep a day streak. |
| **Why now** | Daily shared-result puzzles still hold a large audience: Wordle averaged about 4.05 million daily users in 2025 (Udonis, read November 2026). None of the Minesweeper roguelike listings found mention a shareable daily result (Steam and itch.io listings, November 2026; absence in a listing is not proof). |
| **Lifecycle stage** | Peaking and stable. No end to the window is visible, but no growth either. |
| **Twist** | A muted clip shows a board cleared, then a compact coloured grid stamped out and "Day 12" ticking up. Competitors show a score. |
| **Keep vs change** | Keep the existing daily seed and the "Share this result" button. Change the plain text result into an emoji grid, and add a day streak and a small calendar to the title screen. |
| **Automation lever** | Already there: the daily board comes from the seeded generator and its solver. A "par" line (what the perfect-logic bot scores on today's seed) can be computed in the page. |
| **Social hook** | The grid itself, pasted into chats. Video format: "today's dig in 20 seconds". Second step: a challenge link that carries a seed and a score, so a friend plays the same run. |
| **Monetization** | Weak on its own (web, free). On a portal: a rewarded "second attempt at today's dig". No purchase reason. |
| **MVP scope** | Emoji grid, day streak in the saved profile, title-screen calendar. Under one week. Web only. |
| **Not in MVP** | Leaderboard (needs a server), challenge links, bot par. |
| **Kill switch** | Share taps per daily run finished, and next-day return on the daily. No benchmark found, so **threshold not verified**: add a counter first, take two weeks as your own baseline, and drop the feature if shares stay near zero. |
| **Risks** | 1. Nobody shares a grid they cannot read: test the grid on someone who has not played. 2. `Profile.KEY` must not be bumped for the streak, or every player's progress resets: add the field with a default. |
| **Scores** | 5 · 5 · 4 · 2 · 3 · 3 · 5 = 27/35 |

### Idea: The Jackpot Flip

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Core loop** | Clear the floor → the cache hunt becomes a loud choice: each covered tile shows its odds, your multiplier pulses, and you flip for a payout or walk away → a hit at x5 pays out with a coin fountain and a rising count; a trap breaks the streak on screen. |
| **Why now** | "Minesweeper plus a run" is now common (Infinity Sweeper, May 2026; three more listed for 2026). The gamble on a tile that is a trap or a cache is the part of Deepsweep the others do not describe, and today it is the quietest moment in the game. |
| **Lifecycle stage** | The Minesweeper roguelike niche looks like it is peaking. Estimate: a few months before a twist stops reading as new. |
| **Twist** | A muted three-second clip: multiplier at x5, a finger hovers, flip, the screen erupts in coins. It shows a decision and a payoff with no reading. |
| **Keep vs change** | Keep every rule. Change presentation only: odds on tiles during the hunt (the engine's `cacheOdds` already computes them), a pulsing multiplier, a staged payout, a "walk away" button that names what you bank. |
| **Automation lever** | None needed. Boards and odds are already generated and checked. |
| **Social hook** | The clip above. Shareable output: "x5 cache on the boss floor" card. |
| **Monetization** | On a portal: a rewarded "one more flip" after a trap in the hunt, and a rewarded reroll in the shop. Purchase reason: none yet. |
| **MVP scope** | Hunt-phase UI and effects in `ui.js` only. One to two weeks. Web only. |
| **Not in MVP** | New rules, new items, sound beyond the existing synth. |
| **Kill switch** | Share of cleared floors where the player flips at least one hunt tile, before and after. **Threshold not verified**: needs a counter and your own baseline. |
| **Risks** | 1. Showing odds may make the hunt a solved sum: show them as three bands, not exact numbers. 2. Rule 2 in the project notes says no rules in `ui.js`: keep it to presentation. |
| **Scores** | 4 · 5 · 4 · 3 · 3 · 3 · 5 = 27/35 |

### Idea: Bosses that act

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Core loop** | On the boss floor the boss takes a turn every few flips: the Crusher drops a slab that covers open tiles again, the Jammer turns one clue to static, the Hoarder swallows a cache you have not found → you clear the floor before the boss undoes it. |
| **Why now** | The new portraits give each boss a face, but each boss is still one line of rule text. Competing titles list relic counts (one lists over 100) rather than opponents (Steam listings, November 2026). |
| **Lifecycle stage** | Same niche, peaking. |
| **Twist** | A muted clip shows a portrait lunge and the board change under your finger. |
| **Keep vs change** | Keep the seven bosses and their entry rules. Change: one board action each, on a visible countdown. |
| **Automation lever** | Partial. Each action needs solver support so the dead-end count stays exact, which the project notes require for any new tile or clue type. |
| **Social hook** | Boss-specific clips; "which boss ended your run" on the end card. |
| **Monetization** | Little. A rewarded "stun the boss for three flips" on a portal. |
| **MVP scope** | Two bosses only (Crusher, Jammer) to test the idea. Three to four weeks including the solver work and a rebalance with the simulation bot. |
| **Not in MVP** | The other five bosses, animation beyond a lunge. |
| **Kill switch** | Boss-floor win rate from the simulation bot must stay inside the current range per boss (about 72% to 87% at depth 0, measured this session). If two bosses cannot be balanced in the build window, stop. |
| **Risks** | 1. It can break the no-guess guarantee: solver first, feature second. 2. It is the only idea here that changes balance: every character and depth needs re-measuring. |
| **Scores** | 3 · 3 · 4 · 2 · 4 · 3 · 4 = 23/35 |

## Recommendation

Build the **Daily Dig share card** first. It ships in under a week, changes no rules, and gives you the first thing a player can post. Follow it with **The Jackpot Flip**, which supplies the clip that makes a stranger stop scrolling. Leave **Bosses that act** until the first two show that people come back.

## Not chosen

More relics and build synergies (the crowded part of the field), a server leaderboard (needs a backend), and odd-or-even clue tiles (already on the "not built yet" list and needs solver work).
