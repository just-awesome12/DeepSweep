DEEPSWEEP

Your kit

How to play

  1. A number counts the buried tiles that touch it. A buried tile is a trap or a cache.
  2. Flip every safe tile to clear the floor. A trap costs 1 heart. A cache pays 3 coins.
  3. Safe flips in a row build a streak. Every 8 raises your multiplier, up to x5, and a cache pays that many times over. A trap resets the streak.
  4. Each floor names a row for one cache and a column for the other. After the floor is clear, flip covered tiles to hunt them, or descend.
  5. One gem hides under a safe tile on each floor. It adds 2 to your streak.
  6. A dead end is a point where logic runs out. Guess, or spend an item.
  7. At each fork, pick the steady path or the rich vein: 1 more trap and 1 more dead end, but caches pay double.
  8. Spend coins in the shop between floors. Beat the boss floor to win. Score is every coin you earn plus 5 per heart left. Runs pay stars, and stars buy characters.
